# The company

Source: HealthCore CONTEXT.md company briefing; specialized onboarding briefing stage 2 (The company).

## History

HealthCore is an outpatient healthcare services company founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas. CEO Dr. Sandra Okonkwo founded the company after working inside large hospital systems, with the aim of making high-quality care accessible without long waits or a confusing process.

## Network

HealthCore operates 12 clinics: 9 in the United States (Texas, Florida, and Georgia) and 3 in the United Kingdom (London and Manchester). The company employs approximately 200 people and generates around 28 million dollars in annual revenue.

## Service lines

Clinics offer primary care, specialist consultations, chronic disease management, and preventive health programmes.

## Value proposition

The brand signature is accessible care: same-day bookings, extended hours, and bilingual staff in US markets. Corporate hires must be able to explain that accessibility, same-day appointments, and bilingual care are the documented value proposition, not optional marketing copy.

## Corporate offices in this programme

This onboarding path is for non-clinical corporate staff based in Austin or London. Physicians, nurses, and direct-care clinic staff follow a separate credentialing path that is out of scope here.

## Regulatory setting

HealthCore operates under HIPAA in the United States and UK GDPR in the United Kingdom. Those rules are taught in the compliance stage. This document only records that both frameworks apply to the company as a whole.
