# How we work

Source: specialized onboarding briefing stage 6; HealthCore CONTEXT.md Technology and People briefings.

## Corporate tools

Corporate onboarding in this programme uses:

- Jira for tracked work
- Notion for official written material
- Slack for internal messages
- Microsoft 365 for mail and documents

These tools are not a substitute for systems that store patient information. Access to EHR or claims platforms is a later, gated request.

## Austin and London

Both corporate offices work in English. Austin is in the US HIPAA environment. London is in the UK GDPR environment. Time zones differ; hand-offs should be written in Jira or Notion so the other office does not depend on a same-hour Slack ping. Spanish is optional for Florida clinic contexts and is not provided as official material in this English programme, so policies must not be machine-translated on the fly.

## How a new system or integration is approved

1. Technology (James Osei's line) reviews whether the integration fits the existing estate.
2. If the system would store or display patient information, Compliance (Claire Whitfield) must evaluate it under HIPAA and/or UK GDPR before approval.
3. People is informed when the change creates or removes staff access.
4. No shortcut exists that issues patient-information access because a manager is in a hurry.

A Digital engineer does not connect a new vendor to an EHR as a self-service step.

## After this stage

Stage 7 is access requests for the department's systems and onboarding closure by Diane Foster. Patient-information access still requires stage 4 to have been approved.
