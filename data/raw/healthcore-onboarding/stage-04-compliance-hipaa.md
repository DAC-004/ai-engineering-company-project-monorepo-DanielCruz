# Compliance guidelines (HIPAA)

Source: HealthCore CONTEXT.md Compliance and Data Governance briefing; specialized onboarding briefing stage 4 and Diane Foster's order rule. Applies to Austin and other US-framework corporate hires.

## Why this stage blocks access

No new hire receives access to any system with patient information until they have passed this compliance stage and Claire Whitfield has approved it. Skipping the stage is a legal risk, not an administrative shortcut.

## HIPAA in this company

HealthCore is a US covered environment for clinics in Texas, Florida, and Georgia. Protected health information may be used only as permitted, and only the minimum necessary information may be accessed for a job task.

## Minimum necessary

"Minimum necessary" means a corporate staff member requests or views only the data required for the assigned task. Curiosity, convenience, and "I might need it later" are not permitted reasons to open a patient-information system.

## Incident notification (United States)

For a HIPAA breach affecting unsecured protected health information, HealthCore's documented US notification window in this onboarding material is 60 days. Corporate staff must escalate a suspected incident through Compliance rather than investigating in an untracked channel.

## Access logging

Access to patient-information systems must be attributable. Shared logins and undocumented look-ups are not allowed. Audit trails today are incomplete across EHRs; new access still requires logging and Claire's approval before it is issued.

## Questions this document does not answer

Questions about a specific incident, a named patient, or a rule not written here are not answered from this page. Uncovered compliance questions are routed to Claire Whitfield.
