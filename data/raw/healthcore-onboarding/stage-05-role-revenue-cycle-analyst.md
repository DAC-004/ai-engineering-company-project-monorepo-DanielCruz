# Your role: Revenue Cycle analyst

Source: HealthCore CONTEXT.md Revenue Cycle and Billing briefing; specialized onboarding briefing stage 5. This is onboarding material, not a claims-processing product.

## Where you sit

Revenue Cycle and Billing is led by Tom Callahan. The function gets HealthCore paid for care already provided. In the US that means commercial insurance, Medicare, and Medicaid. In the UK it is a mix of private pay and a small NHS contract.

## What the role is for

The documented US denial rate is 14 percent, more than double a 5 to 8 percent industry average. Coding practices are inconsistent across locations. UK billing is managed separately from US billing, and there is no unified collection view. An analyst in this programme learns those operational facts and the compliance limits on patient data before touching claims systems that contain patient information.

## Who you work with

- Tom Callahan (Revenue Cycle) as the typical direct manager for this variant
- Claire Whitfield (Compliance) before any patient-information or claims-with-PHI system access
- Clinical operations stakeholders only through approved channels after the compliance gate
- People (Diane Foster) for onboarding closure

## 90-day goals

1. Complete compliance at 100 percent with Claire's approval before requesting access to billing or EHR systems that hold patient information.
2. Be able to explain the US denial-rate problem and the split UK billing model from official material.
3. Use only approved corporate tools until access is granted through stage 7.
4. Never store salary, bank, tax identifiers, or background-check details in tickets or prompts.

## Direct manager approval

This stage is not complete until the comprehension check is passed and the assigned direct manager approves. Another department's manager cannot close this stage.
