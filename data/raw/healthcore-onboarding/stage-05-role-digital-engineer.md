# Your role: HealthCore Digital engineer

Source: HealthCore CONTEXT.md Technology and HealthCore Digital briefings; specialized onboarding briefing stage 5. This is onboarding material, not a product roadmap.

## Where you sit

HealthCore Digital is the internal unit created by CEO Dr. Sandra Okonkwo. You report in the Technology line to CTO James Osei. The Austin technology team is small (six people in the company briefing). Corporate Digital engineers may be based in Austin or London; this programme still uses English as the working language.

## What the role is for

The estate is a patchwork: two EHR platforms, a US billing system, a UK billing spreadsheet, phone-based US scheduling, and manual UK diaries. There is no shared data layer. Your role is to build and integrate internal systems that serve those teams without inventing access to patient information before compliance is approved.

## Who you work with

- James Osei (Technology) for technical direction
- Claire Whitfield (Compliance) before any patient-information system is in scope
- Diane Foster (People) for onboarding and access closure
- Department stakeholders such as Revenue Cycle or Patient Experience only after the compliance gate for data access

## 90-day goals

1. Complete every onboarding stage, including compliance with Claire's approval, before requesting patient-information access.
2. Learn the documented patient journey and why no-shows and denials matter to Digital work.
3. Work only on assigned internal systems using approved tools (Jira, Notion, Slack, Microsoft 365).
4. Treat official documentation as the source for company facts; route uncovered questions instead of guessing.

## Direct manager approval

This stage is not complete until the comprehension check is passed and the assigned direct manager approves. The orchestrator cannot approve the role stage.
