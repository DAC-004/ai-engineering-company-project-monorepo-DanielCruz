# Compliance guidelines (UK GDPR)

Source: HealthCore CONTEXT.md Compliance and Data Governance briefing; specialized onboarding briefing stage 4 and Diane Foster's order rule. Applies to London and other UK-framework corporate hires.

## Why this stage blocks access

No new hire receives access to any system with patient information until they have passed this compliance stage and Claire Whitfield has approved it. Skipping the stage is a legal risk, not an administrative shortcut.

## UK GDPR in this company

HealthCore operates clinics in London and Manchester. Health information is special-category data. Processing requires a lawful basis and the additional conditions that apply to health data. The agent will not invent an Article 6 basis that is not written here.

## Minimum necessary

"Minimum necessary" still applies: access only the information required for the assigned corporate task. UK and US clinics do not share one EHR, so copying records between countries is not a self-service step.

## Incident notification (United Kingdom)

For a personal-data breach, HealthCore's documented UK notification window in this onboarding material is 72 hours. Corporate staff must escalate a suspected incident through Compliance. This document does not replace legal notice templates.

## Access logging

Access to patient-information systems must be attributable. Incomplete audit trails in existing EHRs do not relax the rule for new access. Claire Whitfield approves compliance completion before any such access request can be issued.

## Questions this document does not answer

Questions about a specific incident, a named patient, or a rule not written here are not answered from this page. Uncovered compliance questions are routed to Claire Whitfield.
